# Galaxy surface boundaries

Status: resolved
Type: grilling

## Question

Which existing Living Field capabilities remain on the galaxy surface, which belong in Today/Work/Memory/System, and how should the redesigned galaxy connect to those operational surfaces without duplicating responsibility state or conversation behavior?

## Answer

The Galaxy is read-only exploration and navigation. It owns geography,
communities, relationships, semantic zoom, search, focus, contextual
inspection, and lightweight history playback. Today owns the owner journal;
Work owns Responsibility lifecycle and outcomes; Memory owns retained
knowledge and evidence; Routines owns recurring automation; System owns graph
and runtime inspection; Conversations owns conversation behavior and execution.
Galaxy selection may inspect or route to those canonical surfaces but never
mutates or becomes authoritative for their state.
