# Mino Product Language

Mino is a single-owner personal operator. This language distinguishes what
Mino discusses from what it has accepted responsibility for and can prove.

## Language

**Responsibility**:
A durable outcome Mino has accepted ownership of across turns, channels, or
time. It remains open until truthfully closed.
_Avoid_: Task, chat request, job

**Today Journal**:
The owner-wide, chronological projection of meaningful changes in
responsibilities and outcomes for a local calendar day.
_Avoid_: Activity log, dashboard feed, notification list

**Journal Entry**:
The living current projection of one meaningful responsibility, outcome, or
observation in the Today Journal.
_Avoid_: Log line, message, event

**Responsibility History**:
The append-only sequence of accepted, changed, blocked, carried, and verified
events underlying a Responsibility.
_Avoid_: Chat history, editable timeline

**Work**:
The owner-wide portfolio of Responsibilities Mino currently owns, including
working, waiting, blocked, and recently closed responsibilities.
_Avoid_: To-do list, conversation history

**Routine**:
A standing recurring Responsibility whose executions contribute journal and
history events without creating another Responsibility.
_Avoid_: Repeated task, cron job, schedule

**Evidence**:
Runtime-recorded proof supporting a claimed state or outcome, such as a
receipt, artifact, read-back, or external confirmation.
_Avoid_: Explanation, model confidence
